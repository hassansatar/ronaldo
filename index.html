ABOUT CHRISTIANO RONALDO

Welcome to the Official Cristiano Ronaldo Experience!

Christiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro. he was born in Funchal, Portugal on February 5,
1985. he is 40 years old and he's 6'2" or 1.87 meter tall. Known worldwide for
his unmatched work ethic, iconic goals, and dedication to excellence,
Ronaldo continues to inspire millions on and off the pitch.

Cristiano Ronaldo was born on February 5, 1985, in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal, into a modest family.
He is the youngest of four children. His father, José Dinis Aveiro, was a municipal gardener and
part-time kit man for a local football club, and his mother, Maria worked as a cleaner and cook before
eventually opening her own restaurant in Brazil,Casa Aveiro.Meanwhile, José was a gardener and a
part-time kit man for a local soccer team, CF Andorinha. According to The New York Times,
when Ronaldo was 12 years old in the late 1990s, José was the equipment manager for Andorinha's
senior team. Before his career in soccer, though, Ronaldo's dad had been enlisted to fight for Portugal
in the Angolan War of Independence in the '70s, per ESPN. Upon his return, he began working odd
jobs to help support his family.

Career

International Career

Cristiano Ronaldo, born in 1985, began his career in 2002 and became
a global football icon with clubs like Manchester United,
Real Madrid, and Al-Nassr.

Cristiano Ronaldo won two major international trophies with Portugal: the UEFA Euro 2016,
where Portugal defeated France in the final to claim their first-ever major title, and the
UEFA Nations League in 2019, leading his country to victory over the Netherlands in the
inaugural edition of the tournament.
